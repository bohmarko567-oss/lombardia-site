Lombardia Automobili is an independent automotive design house. This artwork is limited to 50 physical prints in total across all sizes, colour variants, framing options and sales channels. STUDIO DI FORMA is made for people who appreciate the design of a car as much as the way it drives.

The subject is the 1972 Pantera, the early coupe from before the safety bumpers, styled by Tom Tjaarda at Ghia. Its mid-mounted Ford 351 Cleveland V8, ZF transaxle and steel monocoque made it a defining De Tomaso, sold in the United States through Lincoln-Mercury dealers. The early Pantera put Italian wedge design and American mechanical force in one car.
