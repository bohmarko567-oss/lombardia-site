04 · How it works
#StageThen
1Sheet waitingthe founder approves the sheet before a dollar is spent
2Sheet approvedpython lotset.py build <car> <coll> --go
3Set generatedthe founder judges every shot on this page
4Set approvedcatawiki-upload in the founder's Chrome, then Submit is his
5Draftedthe founder presses Submit on Catawiki
6SubmittedCatawiki's expert reviews it; nothing to do here
7Not approvedCatawiki's expert declined it; history since the 60 x 80 standard
8Livewatch the auction
