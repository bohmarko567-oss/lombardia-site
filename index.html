04 · How it works
#StageThen
1Sheet waitingthe founder approves the sheet before a dollar is spent
2Artwork approvedbuild the approved artwork's lot images
3Lot builtcheck the selected images before uploading
4Set approvedcatawiki-upload in the founder's Chrome, then Submit is his
5Draftedthe founder presses Submit on Catawiki
6SubmittedCatawiki's expert reviews it; nothing to do here
7Approvedexpert selected the lot; auction scheduling pending
8Not approvedCatawiki's expert declined it; history since the 60 x 80 standard
9Livewatch the auction

macro-v2corner macro
macro-v2-layeredcorner macro · layered

clips-rail-layeredclips on a rail · layered
noir-wallthe noir wall
room-fixedF40 room · layered

framed-gpt2framed · GPT-image-2, v2 references
framed-properframed · GPT-image-2 with Prodigi's frame photographs
framed-trueframed corner · GPT-image-2 with the true-scale frame references
researchresearch case · which model holds the letters
housetwo photographs, third and last of every upload - retired 2026-09-07 (RULES.md 28: no house photograph on any lot)
