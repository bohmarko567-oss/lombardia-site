Lombardia Automobili - standing rules from the founder

Each rule was paid for with a rejection. Read before any lot photo, mockup or scene work.

1. Never paste a flat file onto a generated photograph (2026-09-03)

Banned, permanently, for every kind of lot photo, mockup or scene: compositing the poster file onto a generated image by homography, opaque swap into the field the model painted, luminance gain, drop shadow, cut-paper edge, pasting into a rendered frame opening - any variant.

Founder, on 542 / 311 / 323 and "many, many others": "You just put something on top of the generated photo and you totally mess up the lighting, the angles, it's just slop. On the photos where the poster hangs on a hanger or something it's just so obvious: paste, paste in photo on top. Never do it again. Do it smart. Find a solution."

What stands instead: the image model renders the print inside the scene, so light, angle, paper, clips, tape and hands are all one photograph. Our job is to feed it the true sheet at the highest resolution the API takes and to MEASURE how faithful its rendering is (fidelity.py), so the founder judges from evidence. The true file itself is shown only flat: the hero (01) and the native crops (03, 05, 06).

Retired code and outputs: _attic/BLACKLISTED-paste-2026-09-03/ (WHY.md inside).

One named exception (founder, 2026-09-04): the noir wall. He kept 501 - the bare sheet hung on the dark rooms-ref/noir.png wall by noir.py + hangreal.py at 68 % of the panel - "absolutely love". That one recipe stays in the set. Nothing else pasted, ever.

Amendment, same night, in his words: "In order for it to work the picture has to be layered. You put the picture you want and then you layer on top - tape, a person - so it doesn't look like an obvious paste into a 2D image." So the one composite that is allowed is the LAYERED build, layers.py: the true sheet goes UNDER the model's light (illumination lifted from the model's own rendering, full strength) and UNDER every occluder (tape, clip, fingers, a shoulder, a head, matted out of the photo and laid back on top), with its geometry locked to where the model put the paper by feature matches, and sharpness and grain matched to the photo. Anything that puts the sheet ON TOP of the photograph is still the banned paste. The founder judges whether a layered result passes; nothing is pre-picked.

2. Never warp a poster onto a generated empty table or board (2026-09-03)

The "desk boards" - a generated empty white table at a known angle with the poster projected on by homography. "Absolutely hated." Retired to _attic/BLACKLISTED-desk-boards-2026-09-03/. Rule 1 covers it as well; it is listed on its own because it was hated on its own.

3. All materials go to the founder unpicked (2026-09-03)

"Gimme all the materials to approve first, I can't trust your opinion on this." Every output, including rejects, on the board, labelled. Measurements beside them are welcome; a shortlist is not.

4. Spend needs his go, quoted from estimate-cost, every time

Unchanged. See the memory media-generation-spend-rule.

5. Every generation gets as many high-quality references as the model takes (2026-09-04)

Founder: "for each corner or detail you have to give, and it is a broad rule for any generation actually: you just give as many high-quality references as you can." So: read the model's images_list limit (GET /models/<name> schema; pro-edit 8, nano-banana-2-edit 14) and fill it - the whole sheet, the exact region, a tighter crop of it, the elements in it, the collections around it, a mood photo last. Never one small preview when the API takes eight full crops.

6. What a lot macro is (2026-09-04)

A corner of the print, a third of the sheet in frame, camera about a metre up: ONE thin sheet of paper on a white table, full bleed to the cut edge, hairline edge, no board, no mount, no white border, nothing else on the table (a pencil at true scale at most). "Thick like cardboard" and "a white frame" were the round-1 rejections.

7. What the house showcase is (2026-09-04)

The real studio mix - engineering sheets AND nativo (campo, editoriale, pianta on every ground) AND the style studies - a random mix, one or two big in the middle, different ones on the sides, like an exhibition hang. Half the shots with no people. Shot like a professional interiors photographer: full-frame camera, prime lens, level horizon, some blur. "You didn't use the mix from the studio" was the round-1 rejection.

8. The pipeline has two gates and both are the founder's (2026-09-04)

"First I'd have to approve manually the poster that goes through the lot pipeline so there is no waste on generations of bad posters." So lotset.py build refuses until approve-sheet has recorded his words, and nothing goes to Catawiki until he has approved each shot on the board. The set itself is fixed: flat truth, four corner macros, table hero flat and side, clips wall raw and layered, noir wall, plus the three house photographs. Change the set only on his word.

Restated 2026-09-05, when the collections started to widen: "before you go with the full lot photos, I just need heroes to approve. That's a rule." So a new car's path is: sheet built -> lotset.py register -> on the board as sheet-pending -> his approval -> only then lotset.py build. The only spend before his approval is the car render itself (about $0.36 a car).

9. The site is the workplace, nothing else lives on it (2026-09-04)

"Perfect structure for work, no junk or garbage, well designed workplace." One pipeline index, one page per poster, the house, the rules, and a Lab page that only links the tools that make a sheet. Experiments, rejects and contact sheets never reach the site; the attic keeps them.

10. The hero is the layered r4 room, never a generated hero (2026-09-04)

Founder: "I set room-r4-pro-1 as a hero and I want it as a hero for every lot, since it can be layered instead of being generated, so we save money and never have a chance of losing a detail on a hero." So for every poster after the two F40s: the set has no table hero; lotset.py build takes the r4 room (architecture office, pro) and lays the TRUE sheet under the model's light with layers.py, measured by fidelity.py, as hero-room-r4-layered. Free, and the print is the file itself. The two F40s stay as he ordered them.

11. The house holds photo 3 and photo 6 of every upload (2026-09-04)

Founder: "as a rule when uploading each 3rd and last photos are house photos. You can put them in the list and lock em there." And: "by last photo I meant last, we upload all of them, not just 6." So everything he kept goes up, in his order, with house photograph one (the table spread) third and house photograph two (along the wall) last. The board locks those two slots; lotset.py writes the upload list into the state on every apply; mk-board.py zips the full-size files in that order (site/download/<key>-full-size.zip, "Full size for posting"); catawiki-upload follows it. Files go up uncompressed: the 3000 px q92 JPEGs of the set, never the board's thumbnails.

12. Two sheets, two ways to make them, nothing older (2026-09-05)

Founder: "You had to take the two that are online right now. The red one and the blue one. That's it. Take exactly them, exactly the skills, exactly how we created them, and duplicate. ... We worked hard on the blue and the red collection. We don't need old shit to get in the way."

  • Red = the Ossido F40 online (lot 106603826): python sheet.py <car> ossido landscape, md5 37ed0df5049cdb41d0c40709badfd088 reproduces it. The car is regenerated, the lines are drawn.
  • Blue = the blue F40 online (lot 106624824): python blue.py <car>, md5 54ce32e44c54b8c540c27355e28a4799 reproduces it. Full-bleed ground, one outer rule, spec box 120 px off the east and south rules, LA + TAV + signature in a row above it.
  • Everything older - lot.py's blueprint / ossido / notte plates, the generated-whole sheets, the style lab - is retired to _attic/RETIRED-2026-09-05-old-collections/ and is never built from again. lot.py refuses without --retired-ok. A new car is a duplicate of one of the two, never a reproduction of anything older.
  • Prove it every time an engine file changes: rebuild both F40s and compare the md5s.

13. One house box for every Ossido hero; long, low cars for the blue (2026-09-05)

Founder: "they all in ossido have to be placed exactly like F40 in matters of size and x y" - so every car after the F40 is contained in the F40's own box (poster.HOUSE_HERO_BOX, the approved sheet's box after its scale and shift); a car's own source box is no longer used. A raised scissor door or anything that lifts the silhouette above the F40's runs into the header, so the Countach is drawn doors closed. "Feels empty" was the smaller boxes.

Founder on the blue 2CV and T1: "way too small and leave too much empty estate. I think that's a car choice problem." A car taller than about a third of its length cannot be drawn at 1:10 in the blue layout and looks lost at 1:12 or 1:15. Blue takes long, low cars; the two were taken off the board. The first-batch 911 (930) "looks great because you draw a lot of detail" - dense traced figures are what fills a blue sheet; a sparse base makes an empty one.

14. The spec-box title band, the dimension lines, the density of a blue plate (2026-09-05)

From the founder's first board round on the new cars: - A typeset title band is fitted to its text and the text is centred on its ink ("text is all over the height there... the box a bit too tall... save as a future rule"). 0.22 of the block for a typeset name; the asset band (F40 badge) keeps 0.30. - Hero dimension lines are anchored to the car, never copied from a source at its length. PASSO a fixed gap below the tyre contacts, LUNGHEZZA further out between nose and tail, LARGHEZZA along the bumper edge in front of the nose between the car's own front extremes ("2504 has to go a bit farther from the wheels, south-east; the 1760 line is way too long, overshoots 3 or 4x, over the bumper - south-west"). poster.draw_hero_dims_anchored; the F40 keeps the 362 system. - A blue plate is dense or it is "too simple". The F40 plate is a patent drawing, ~6.2 % ink; bases from gen-nano.mjs came back as outlines at 4.5-5.6 % and were rejected on sight. Generate with --dense (parts-diagram density) and check measure-base.py before tracing. - A short car fills the sheet at a larger scale - the Stratos (3710 mm) at 1:8, not 1:10; the ladder in patentsheet2 says which ratio still fits. - A marque without a period vector is typeset and he is told so (Lancia: the only 1974 mark on Commons is a 489 px badge photograph). The Pagoda carries the Mercedes-Benz serif wordmark at his request for "a font or a style", knowing it is the 1990s cut.

15. The lot set: full reference slots, a clean layered hero, honest lettering (2026-09-05)

Founder, on the 930 set: "very sloppy work - either generations were unlucky or you didn't use all the available reference slots. the hero image has this weird blue glow around the drawn lines. most of the close-up shots and other shots are just gibberish text."

  • Every images_list is full. pro-edit takes 8, nano-banana-2-edit 14. A macro sends the 4k sheet, its corner at 45 / 36 / 28 / 18 %, the three elements in it, the table, then the other corners. gen-macro.mjs logs refs n/limit per job; a job under the limit is a bug.
  • The layered hero is built from a pre-shrunk sheet, with the light read off the ground as a luminance field and one flat colour cast, and a quarter of the model's grain. The 3x minification inside the warp aliased every line into a sparkle; per-channel light bent the hue of every line edge; full grain read as a speckled halo. (layers.py.)
  • Lettering on a macro is copied letter for letter or the shot is dropped. The prompt says so; the reference crops show the text large. A layered macro twin is admitted only with a solid lock (250+ matches, under 15 % occluded); a loose lock warps the print and is worse than the render.
  • The board saves a note on every keystroke, and keys typed into a field never reach the shortcuts. A note followed by Keep / Drop / Next used to die with the textarea before its change event fired; then a, k, r, x and the arrow keys typed INTO the note toggled verdicts and jumped shots ("notes still don't work"). A board test types a real word like "artifact" plus an arrow key.

16. Rooms are the fixed set from the two F40 lots, layered, never generated (2026-09-05)

Founder: "as the rule you only use fixed rooms that we fixed with previous posters - F40 Ossido and F40 Archivio Tecnico. That's it. Rule, skill, remember forever."

  • Red (Ossido): the rooms he kept on the Ossido F40 - room-r4-pro-1 (the cover), room-r6-pro-1, room-r2-nb2-1. Blue (Archivio Tecnico): the rooms he kept on the blue F40 - room-r1-pro-1 (the cover), room-r4-pro-1, room-r6-pro-1, room-r2-nb2-1, room-r6-nb2-1. The renders live in lots/f40-<collection>/_gen/rooms/.
  • Every new sheet is LAYERED into those exact photographs (layers.py --ref-sheet <F40 sheet> --sheet <new sheet> <room png>): the lock, the light and the occluders come from the F40 sheet the room was rendered with; the new sheet is painted through the same geometry. 600-800 matches against the reference, against 60-180 on a fresh render. No room is ever generated again: $0.78 a poster saved, and no detail can be lost or invented.
  • The hero of every lot stays the r4 room, layered (rule 10), from this fixed set.

17. A layered room passes two fresh critics before it reaches the board (2026-09-05)

Founder, on the first fixed rooms: "those rooms were rejected because the poster was inserted with light and other artifacts. do better and use judges or loops."

  • Before any layered room or hero goes on the board, a judge set is written (_serve/judge*/: the raw render, the composite, a 100 % crop of the header and of a corner) and TWO fresh-context critics (sonnet, independent, the rubric in the lot-photos skill) grade every room PASS or FAIL with the exact place of each defect. A FAIL is fixed in the compositing and re-judged; nothing is hand-picked past a FAIL. The founder still judges last, on the board.
  • What the critics and the 100 % crops caught this night, now in layers.py: the ink mask covers letter INTERIORS, not only edges (thick wordmarks came out pink or cyan inside with white rims); in fixed-room mode only non-print colours are occluders (the other poster's redraw is never an object, it came back as white smears); the paint is clipped to the model's own paper (a blue tab stuck out past the edge); no unsharp mask (dark rings around bright letters); highlights compressed so glare never blows the lettering out.
  • Later the same night, from the 100 % corner crops: the sheet's footprint grows to the model's paper (a band of flat room-blue with no grid showed along two edges); the paint is clipped to the widened colour quad of the print plus the core of any LARGE occluder (a car, a hand), never a small pale blob; occluders are closed and hole-filled so an object's highlights cannot punch through; the paper edge is crisp against the wall and feathered only under an out-of-focus object. Every one of these was found on a 100 % crop of a corner, never at page size.
  • The hero of a lot is the collection's own F40 cover room (red r4, blue r1): the r4 office holds a Ferrari model, off-look under any other marque.
  • The board: the lot in order is the one judging place (keep / drop / note per row, kept ones draggable), the separate judge grid and the swipe review are gone, every note is listed beside Send.

18. Layering works only on a straight-on photograph with nothing in the way (2026-09-05)

Founder, on the 930 set, in his words: "hero-room-r1-layered works. wall-clips-layered-1 works. room-r6-pro-1 works by luck - these need to be generated carefully with detailed and many reference details. hero-room-r4-layered - layering doesn't work here, sucks, artifacts. bad gen, bad photo - period, no layering on these. The ones it works with are straight photos with nothing in the way, those we can keep, others - no. room-r6-nb2-fixed - same problem with layering on a non-layer image, artifacts. drop this forever. room-r6-pro-fixed2 and room-r6-nb2-fixed2 same thing. room-r4-pro-1 - what you had to do is take the original photo from the F40's and layer on there, never regen these; regen broke it."

His answers the same evening, asked on the board rebuild: "layer room-r4-pro-1 and r1 and r2 and wall-clips-layered-1, rest very good gens with references"; "you just get F40 r4 and layer future posters, very simple"; "we shall have rooms that are great for layering and we just reuse the exact same rooms, and then some we generate"; and on the 930's loft: "drop it too".

  • Layered, from the F40 originals he kept: r4-pro (office), r1-pro (living room), r2-nb2 (brick loft) on blue; r4-pro and r2-nb2 on red - plus the clips wall. FIXED_ROOMS in lotset.py. The hero is the collection's F40 cover room, layered: red r4, blue r1 (rule 10, 17).
  • Generated, carefully: the garage (r6, one per model), every reference slot full - the 4k sheet, its corners, the header, the title block (rule 5). GEN_ROOMS in lotset.py; gen-unframed.mjs --set=rooms --only=r6 --refs=<dir>. "Works by luck" is not a recipe; the references are. r6-nb2 layered is dropped forever; r6 is never layered.
  • The 930's r4 WAS the blue F40's own r4 photograph with the 930 sheet layered in (619 feature matches, source png byte-identical to lots/f40-tecnico/_gen/rooms/pro-r4-1.png), not a regenerated room. What he saw and dropped were layering artifacts on that angled print with the model in front. So r4 stays in the set on his word, and every layered r4 passes the rule-17 judge loop at 100 % crops before it reaches the board - or it does not reach it.
  • The cover room is emitted once. The 930 carried room-r1-pro-fixed and hero-room-r1-layered as the same bytes under two ids; the build now skips the fixed copy of the hero room.
  • Everything generated is shown. room-r1-pro-1 and room-r6-nb2-1 (the 930's own generated living room and garage) were dropped by the pipeline under rule 16, never by him - back on the board as waiting on his word ("show em if they exist").

19. The poster page: hero, the lot in order (in by default), the photographs, the stores (2026-09-05)

Founder: "first the big hero, then 02 with order and accept by default / reject option with notes, clear and responsive, then 03 - just a pool of photos. All approving and tossing happens in 02. Drop the artifact / excessive buttons, leave just the note window and make it work properly - you leave the note, press a button and it gets over; make it clear. Make the page laconic. Get rid of 'full size for posting'; a separate download button on each photo in 02 and 03. Keep the Catawiki preview. On the board, every uploaded poster appears separately below the ones that need work, and a card opens the lot page, never Catawiki - the store link lives inside the lot page, one section, one link per store."

  • 01 the sheet, big. The gate (Approve / Not yet + note) while it waits for him; a chip after.
  • 02 the lot, in order. Every shot is IN unless he drops it. Per row: number (cover first), the photograph, id, a note field that saves on every keystroke ("saved" tick), a download of the full-size file, drag handle, up/down, Drop. The house photographs are locked third and last. Dropped rows sit below under "Dropped · N" with Restore. The preview "as Catawiki shows it" stays. On Send, every untouched shot becomes an explicit keep, so the pipeline and the page agree.
  • 03 the photographs. The whole pool, dropped ones dimmed, the house at the end; a download on each. No dropping here; a dropped photograph carries Restore (↶) on its tile and in its lightbox (his ask, the same night: "add restore option to photographs section").
  • Drag is real. The handle drags a row; at the top or bottom edge of the screen the page scrolls on its own while the finger holds, the row stays under the finger, the target is marked as it passes ("when I drag and reach the end of screen the scroll has to begin automatically").
  • 04 print file (when there is one) · 05 stores · 06 history (collapsed). Stores: one row per store with its state and one link (Catawiki now; others later). Listing text collapsed under it.
  • Send is unmistakable. The bottom bar says what is new ("2 decisions · order changed · not sent yet") and lists the notes; after Send it turns green - "Sent to Claude · 18:23 · 2 decisions · 1 note · via ntfy+mail" - and stays until he taps OK. No zip; site/full/<key>/<id>.jpg is the file as it goes up.
  • No piles of text. No hints under section labels, no counts in prose, no deck paragraphs.
