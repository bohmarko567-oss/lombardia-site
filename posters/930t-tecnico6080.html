Porsche 911 Turbo, Archivio Tecnico, Tavola I. Limited edition, framed.

This 911 Turbo is drawn as a four-view engineering plate - side, front, rear and plan - with its dimension lines and a title block on a blueprint-blue ground. The title block carries the factory figures for the 1978 car: 4291 x 1775 x 1311 mm on a 2272 mm wheelbase, 3299 cc 6-Zyl. Boxer, 300 PS, 260 km/h.

This one comes framed: a white frame, a slim 20 mm profile with mitred corners, chosen to lift the blueprint blue. 60 x 80 cm, 600 x 800 mm print, 630 x 830 mm framed.

Every sheet is signed. Printed on a smooth satin stock. Shipped flat with its frame, corners protected, in a rigid box; the print drops into the frame and it is ready to hang.

Archivio Tecnico is our blue series: one ground, one ink, one drawing standard.
