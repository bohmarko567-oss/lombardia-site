E-type Series 1 3.8 FHC - FORMA / SCULPTURE - 80 × 60 cm - 1of200
Description

You’re bidding on an 80 × 60 cm FORMA / SCULPTURE poster by Lombardia Automobili, featuring the 1961 Jaguar E-type Series 1 3.8 FHC. The artwork is 100% unique combination of a detailed illustration of the car and our own layout and typography. A white frame and Perspex glazing are included; poster and frame are shipped together with care.

Deep green, pale ivory and oversized E-type typography give the artwork presence across a room. The pale green Jaguar and its flowing side profile invite a closer look. Above a desk or in a garage lounge, it’s a conversation piece for anyone who knows what that name means.

Lombardia Automobili is an independent automotive design house. FORMA / SCULPTURE limited edition 1of200 is made for people who appreciate the design of a car as much as the way it drives.

The subject is the 1961 E-type Series 1 3.8 FHC, the fixed-head coupé from Jaguar’s original E-type range. Its unmistakable long bonnet, 3.8-litre straight-six and flowing roofline made it a defining Jaguar. The E-type was unveiled in Geneva in March 1961.

Sheet size: 80 × 60 cm. Nominal assembled frame dimensions: 83 × 63 cm. This is a contemporary, retro-inspired studio edition.

An independent artwork by Lombardia Automobili, not affiliated with or endorsed by Jaguar Land Rover Limited.
