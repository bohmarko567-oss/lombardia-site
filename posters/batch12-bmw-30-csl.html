3.0 CSL - SPORT / LEICHT - 45 × 60 cm - 1of200
Description

You’re bidding on a 45 × 60 cm SPORT / LEICHT poster by Lombardia Automobili, featuring the 1973 BMW 3.0 CSL. The artwork is 100% unique combination of a detailed illustration of the car and our own layout and typography. A white frame and Perspex glazing are included; poster and frame are shipped together with care.

Warm ivory, Motorsport stripes and oversized vertical CSL typography give the artwork presence across a room. The silver CSL and its technical details invite a closer look. Above a desk or in a garage lounge, it’s a conversation piece for anyone who knows what those three letters mean.

Lombardia Automobili is an independent automotive design house. SPORT / LEICHT limited edition 1of200 is made for people who appreciate the design of a car as much as the way it drives.

The subject is the 1973 3.0 CSL, created as a homologation special. Its straight-six engine, lightweight construction and distinctive aerodynamic package made it a defining BMW. The prominent rear wing earned this version the nickname Batmobile.

Sheet size: 45 × 60 cm. Nominal assembled frame dimensions: 48 × 63 cm. This is a contemporary, retro-inspired studio edition.

An independent artwork by Lombardia Automobili, not affiliated with or endorsed by BMW AG.
