Giulia TZ — Studio di Forma, Landscape
Description · 4 paragraphs

AI-assisted automotive artwork by Lombardia Automobili, 2026. Giulia TZ, from the Studio di Forma collection.

Two red views of the 1963 Alfa Romeo Giulia TZ on a warm ivory ground, with tall red lettering and restrained technical details. A large front three-quarter illustration anchors the lower left. A smaller rear view sits to the right, overlapping the red TZ lettering.

80 × 60 cm print, specified with a white budget frame and no mount. Nominal outside dimensions: 83 × 63 cm.

Studio di Forma pairs the car’s sculpted bodywork with clear typography and generous paper space.
