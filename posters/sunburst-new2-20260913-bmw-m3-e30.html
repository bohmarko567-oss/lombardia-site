Lot title
M3 E30 - FORMA / MOTION - 45 × 60 cm - Edition of 100
Description · 6 paragraphs

You’re bidding on a 45 × 60 cm FORMA / MOTION poster by Lombardia Automobili, featuring the 1988 BMW M3 E30. The artwork is 100% unique combination of a detailed illustration of the car and our own layout and typography. A white frame and Perspex glazing are included; poster and frame are shipped together with care.

Black, warm ivory and oversized M3 typography give the artwork presence across a room. Diagonal blue and red bands bring a sense of speed to the detailed black coupe, while its four round headlights and silver basketweave wheels invite a closer look. Above a desk or in a garage lounge, it’s a conversation piece for anyone who appreciates the E30’s distinctive shape.

Lombardia Automobili is an independent automotive design house. This artwork is limited to 100 physical prints in total across all sizes, colour variants, framing options and sales channels. FORMA / MOTION is made for people who appreciate the design of a car as much as the way it drives.

The subject is the 1988 BMW M3 E30 road coupe. Its box-shaped wheel arches, four round headlights and raised rear wing give the model an unmistakable identity. The artwork depicts a black car with silver basketweave wheels.

Sheet size: 45 × 60 cm. Nominal assembled frame dimensions: 48 × 63 cm. This is a contemporary, retro-inspired studio edition.

An independent artwork by Lombardia Automobili, not affiliated with or endorsed by BMW.
