Ferrari F40, Serie Ossido, Tavola II. Limited edition.

This F40 is drawn as a large three-quarter study with its front, side and rear elevations beside it, projection lines and dimension arrows. Oxide-red ground, bone-white line, A1, 841 x 594 mm. The title block carries the factory figures for the 1987 car: 4430 x 1970 x 1124 mm on a 2450 mm wheelbase, 2936 cc twin-turbo V8, 478 CV, 321 km/h.

Every sheet is signed. Printed on exclusive archival paper and shipped rolled in a rigid tube with care.

Serie Ossido is our red series: one ground, one ink, one drawing standard.
