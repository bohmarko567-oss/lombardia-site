Lot title
Porsche 911 GT3 RS 2023 Poster, Arctic Grey - Supercar Art Print, Framed or Unframed
Description · 12 paragraphs

Porsche 911 GT3 RS 2023 poster by Lombardia Automobili. Giclée art print, shipped rolled or framed and ready to hang. A gift for anyone who loves the Porsche 911 GT3 RS.

- Giclée on 200 gsm fine art paper, archival pigment inks - 4 sizes, from 40 x 30 cm to 100 x 75 cm (16 x 12 in to 40 x 30 in) - Unframed, or in a solid wood frame in 8 colours, ready to hang - Free shipping to the EU, UK, US and Australia - US orders are made and shipped in the US: no import fees - Arrives damaged? Replaced at no cost

Silver-white light streaks flowing through a black studio like smoke in a wind tunnel, with the white GT3 RS lettering bottom left, give the artwork presence across a room. The Arctic Grey 911, low and seen from its front corner with its swan-neck rear wing and red GT3 RS decals, invites a closer look. Above a desk or in a garage lounge, it's a conversation piece for anyone who knows what that name means.

The subject is the 2023 Porsche 911 GT3 RS (992) in Arctic Grey, with red GT3 RS decals, satin black wheels, red brake calipers, the swan-neck rear wing and the vents in its bonnet and front wings. Its 4.0-litre flat-six revs to 9,000 rpm, and it was the first road 911 with a single central radiator in the nose and a drag reduction system in the rear wing.

Every car was a drawing before it was a car. We start with the finished car and draw it back to that first decision, until the proportion is honest and the stance is right and you would know it from across the room without a badge.

THE PRINT Giclée on 200 gsm enhanced matte fine art paper, archival pigment inks. Landscape, 4:3. Choose the size in the menu: 40 x 30 cm (16 x 12 in), 60 x 45 cm (24 x 18 in), 80 x 60 cm (32 x 24 in) or 100 x 75 cm (40 x 30 in).

FRAMED Solid wood classic frame, satin-laminated, 20 mm face, 22 mm deep, Perspex glazing, no mount. Black, white, natural oak, brown, dark grey, light grey, antique silver or antique gold. Arrives assembled with the hanger fitted, ready to hang, flat in a rigid box with the corners protected. Framed outer size is the print size plus about 1.5 cm on each side.

UNFRAMED Shipped rolled in a rigid tube.

DELIVERY Dispatched within 3 to 6 business days, then tracked shipping to your door. Returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery; the buyer pays the return postage. Anything that arrives damaged is reprinted at no cost.

COLOUR Colours are printed from the original artwork file. Paper and screens differ a little, so allow for a slight difference.

QUESTIONS Which size should I choose? 40 x 30 cm suits a shelf or a small wall, 60 x 45 cm sits above a desk or sideboard, 80 x 60 cm and 100 x 75 cm fill the wall above a sofa or bed. Is a frame included? Only if you pick a frame colour. "No frame (rolled)" is the print on its own. Can it be sent as a gift? Yes, enter the recipient's address at checkout and it ships straight to them.

The year and the details describe the car. This is a contemporary artwork by an independent design house, not a period factory document, and not affiliated with Porsche.
