Lot title
Viper RT/10 - AMERICAN FORM - 80 × 60 cm - Edition of 77
Description · 6 paragraphs

You’re bidding on a 80 × 60 cm AMERICAN FORM poster by Lombardia Automobili, featuring the 1992 Dodge Viper RT/10. The artwork is 100% unique combination of a detailed illustration of the car and our own layout and typography. A white frame and Perspex glazing are included; poster and frame are shipped together with care.

Acid green, black and oversized Viper RT/10 typography give the artwork presence across a room. The red Viper and its technical details invite a closer look. Above a desk or in a garage lounge, it’s a conversation piece for anyone who knows what that name means.

Lombardia Automobili is an independent automotive design house. This artwork is limited to 77 physical prints in total across all sizes, colour variants, framing options and sales channels. AMERICAN FORM is made for people who appreciate the design of a car as much as the way it drives.

The subject is the 1992 Viper RT/10, the first production version of Dodge’s uncompromising roadster. Its V10 engine, open cockpit and exposed side exhausts made it a defining Dodge. The RT/10 put its emphasis on a direct, open-air driving experience.

Sheet size: 80 × 60 cm. Nominal assembled frame dimensions: 83 × 63 cm. This is a contemporary, retro-inspired studio edition.

An independent artwork by Lombardia Automobili, not affiliated with or endorsed by Dodge.
