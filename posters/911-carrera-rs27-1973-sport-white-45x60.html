Lot title
Lombardia Automobili - Porsche - 911 Carrera RS 2.7 Sport - SPORT / LEICHT - 45 × 60 cm - 1of200 - 1970s
Description · 6 paragraphs

You’re bidding on a 45 × 60 cm SPORT / LEICHT poster by Lombardia Automobili, featuring the 1973 Porsche 911 Carrera RS 2.7 Sport. The artwork is 100% unique combination of a detailed illustration of the car and our own layout and typography. A white frame and Perspex glazing are included; poster and frame are shipped together with care.

Deep green, warm ivory and oversized 911 typography give the artwork presence across a room. The white-and-green Carrera and its technical details invite a closer look. Above a desk or in a garage lounge, it’s a conversation piece for anyone who knows what those three numbers mean.

Lombardia Automobili is an independent automotive design house. SPORT / LEICHT limited edition 1of200 is made for people who appreciate the design of a car as much as the way it drives.

The subject is the 1973 Carrera RS 2.7, created as a homologation special. Its unmistakable ducktail, 210 PS flat-six and focus on lightness made it a defining Porsche. Just 200 examples were built with the M471 Sport package.

Sheet size: 45 × 60 cm. Nominal assembled frame dimensions: 48 × 63 cm. This is a contemporary, retro-inspired studio edition.

An independent artwork by Lombardia Automobili, not affiliated with or endorsed by Porsche AG.
