Giulia TZ — Studio di Forma, Portrait
Description

AI-assisted automotive artwork by Lombardia Automobili, 2026. Giulia TZ, from the Studio di Forma collection.

Two red views of the 1963 Alfa Romeo Giulia TZ on a warm ivory ground, with tall red lettering and restrained technical details. A front three-quarter illustration sits above a rear three-quarter view, with a compact specification line near the foot of the sheet.

45 × 60 cm print, specified with a white budget frame and no mount. Nominal outside dimensions: 48 × 63 cm.

Studio di Forma pairs the car’s sculpted bodywork with clear typography and generous paper space.
