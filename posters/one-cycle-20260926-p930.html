Porsche 911 Turbo 930 1985 Poster, Guards Red - Sports Car Art Print, Framed or Unframed
Description · 12 paragraphs

Porsche 911 Turbo (930), by Lombardia Automobili.

- Giclée on 200 gsm fine art paper, archival pigment inks - 4 sizes, from 40 x 30 cm to 100 x 75 cm (16 x 12 in to 40 x 30 in) - Unframed, or in a solid wood frame in 8 colours, ready to hang - Printed to order, free shipping to the EU, UK, US and Australia - Arrives damaged? Replaced at no cost

Guards Red, fresh alpine snow and a deep blue winter sky give the artwork presence across a room. The red 911 Turbo and the snow dust behind its rear wheels invite a closer look. Above a desk or in a garage lounge, it's a conversation piece for anyone who knows what that name means.

The subject is a 1985 911 Turbo 3.3, the 930, on a high alpine pass after fresh snow. Built from 1978 to 1989, the 3.3 Turbo had an intercooled, turbocharged flat-six of 300 PS, wide rear arches and a big rear wing. It is the car that made the word turbo a promise.

Every car was a drawing before it was a car. We start with the finished car and draw it back to that first decision, until the proportion is honest and the stance is right and you would know it from across the room without a badge.

THE PRINT Giclée on 200 gsm enhanced matte fine art paper, archival pigment inks. Landscape, 4:3. Choose the size in the menu: 40 x 30 cm (16 x 12 in), 60 x 45 cm (24 x 18 in), 80 x 60 cm (32 x 24 in) or 100 x 75 cm (40 x 30 in).

FRAMED Solid wood classic frame, satin-laminated, 20 mm face, 22 mm deep, Perspex glazing, no mount. Black, white, natural oak, brown, dark grey, light grey, antique silver or antique gold. Arrives assembled with the hook fitted, ready to hang, flat in a rigid box with the corners protected. Framed outer size is the print size plus about 3 cm on each side.

UNFRAMED Shipped rolled in a rigid tube.

DELIVERY Printed to order and dispatched within 3 to 5 business days, then tracked shipping to your door. We cannot take returns, but anything that arrives damaged is replaced at no cost.

COLOUR Colours are printed from the original artwork file. Paper and screens differ a little, so allow for a slight difference.

QUESTIONS Which size should I choose? 40 x 30 cm suits a shelf or a small wall, 60 x 45 cm sits above a desk or sideboard, 80 x 60 cm and 100 x 75 cm fill the wall above a sofa or bed. Is a frame included? Only if you pick a frame colour. "No frame (rolled)" is the print on its own. Can it be sent as a gift? Yes, enter the recipient's address at checkout and it ships straight to them.

The year and the details describe the car. This is a contemporary artwork by an independent design house, not a period factory document, and not affiliated with Porsche AG.
