Lot title
Stratos HF Stradale - FORMA / MOTION - 45 × 60 cm - 1of200
Description

You’re bidding on a 45 × 60 cm FORMA / MOTION poster by Lombardia Automobili, featuring the 1973 Lancia Stratos HF Stradale. The artwork is 100% unique combination of a detailed illustration of the car and our own layout and typography. A white frame and Perspex glazing are included; poster and frame are shipped together with care.

Golden yellow, deep charcoal and oversized STRATOS typography give the artwork presence across a room. The red Stratos and its sculpted bodywork invite a closer look. Above a desk or in a garage lounge, it’s a conversation piece for anyone who knows what that name means.

Lombardia Automobili is an independent automotive design house. FORMA / MOTION limited edition 1of200 is made for people who appreciate the design of a car as much as the way it drives.

The subject is the 1973 Stratos HF Stradale, the road-going counterpart to Lancia’s rally car. Its unmistakable wedge, mid-mounted Dino V6 and compact proportions made it a defining Lancia. The body was designed by Marcello Gandini for Bertone.

Sheet size: 45 × 60 cm. Nominal assembled frame dimensions: 48 × 63 cm. This is a contemporary, retro-inspired studio edition.

An independent artwork by Lombardia Automobili, not affiliated with or endorsed by Lancia.
