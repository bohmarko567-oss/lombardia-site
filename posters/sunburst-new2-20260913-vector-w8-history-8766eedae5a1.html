You’re bidding on a 80 × 60 cm AMERICAN FORM poster by Lombardia Automobili, featuring the 1991 Vector W8. The artwork is 100% unique combination of a detailed illustration of the car and our own layout and typography. A white frame and Perspex glazing are included; poster and frame are shipped together with care.

Deep purple, warm ivory and angular VECTOR typography give the artwork presence across a room. The long, low purple wedge sits beneath fine technical linework, with silver wheels and a broad rear wing inviting a closer look. Above a desk or in a garage lounge, it’s a conversation piece for anyone drawn to unconventional American car design.

Lombardia Automobili is an independent automotive design house. This artwork is limited to 77 physical prints in total across all sizes, colour variants, framing options and sales channels. AMERICAN FORM is made for people who appreciate the design of a car as much as the way it drives.

The subject is the 1991 Vector W8 Twin Turbo road coupe. Its low wedge-shaped body, broad rear wing and slanted side intakes give the model a distinctive presence. The artwork depicts the purple production W8 rather than the earlier W2 prototype.

Sheet size: 80 × 60 cm. Nominal assembled frame dimensions: 83 × 63 cm. This is a contemporary, retro-inspired studio edition.

An independent artwork by Lombardia Automobili, not affiliated with or endorsed by Vector.
