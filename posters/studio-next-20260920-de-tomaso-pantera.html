Lot title
Pantera - STUDIO DI FORMA - 80 × 60 cm - Edition of 50
Description · 6 paragraphs

You’re bidding on an 80 × 60 cm STUDIO DI FORMA poster by Lombardia Automobili, featuring the 1972 De Tomaso Pantera. The artwork is 100% unique combination of a detailed illustration of the car and our own layout and typography. A white frame and Perspex glazing are included; poster and frame are shipped together with care.

Burnt orange, deep chocolate shadow and expressive PANTERA lettering give the artwork presence across a room. The silver Pantera seen from front and rear, its grille badge, script and cabin invite a closer look. Above a desk or in a garage lounge, it’s a conversation piece for anyone who knows what that name means.

Lombardia Automobili is an independent automotive design house. This artwork is limited to 50 physical prints in total across all sizes, colour variants, framing options and sales channels. STUDIO DI FORMA is made for people who appreciate the design of a car as much as the way it drives.

The subject is the 1972 Pantera, the early coupe from before the safety bumpers, styled by Tom Tjaarda at Ghia. Its mid-mounted Ford 351 Cleveland V8, ZF transaxle and steel monocoque made it a defining De Tomaso, sold in the United States through Lincoln-Mercury dealers. The early Pantera put Italian wedge design and American mechanical force in one car.

Sheet size: 80 × 60 cm. Nominal assembled frame dimensions: 83 × 63 cm. This is a contemporary, retro-inspired studio edition.

An independent artwork by Lombardia Automobili, not affiliated with or endorsed by De Tomaso.

Every car was a drawing before it was a car. We start with the finished car and draw it back to that first decision, until the proportion is honest and the stance is right and you would know it from across the room without a badge.

THE PRINT Giclée on 200 gsm enhanced matte fine art paper, archival pigment inks. Landscape, 4:3. Choose the size in the menu: 40 x 30 cm (16 x 12 in), 60 x 45 cm (24 x 18 in), 80 x 60 cm (32 x 24 in) or 100 x 75 cm (40 x 30 in).

FRAMED Solid wood classic frame, satin-laminated, 20 mm face, 22 mm deep, Perspex glazing, no mount. Black, white, natural oak, brown, dark grey, light grey, antique silver or antique gold. Arrives assembled with the hook fitted, ready to hang, flat in a rigid box with the corners protected. Framed outer size is the print size plus about 3 cm on each side.

UNFRAMED Shipped rolled in a rigid tube.

DELIVERY Printed to order and dispatched within 3 to 5 business days, then tracked shipping to your door. We cannot take returns, but anything that arrives damaged is replaced at no cost.
