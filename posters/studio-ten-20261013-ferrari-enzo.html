Lot title
Ferrari Enzo 2003 Poster, Red and Black - Supercar Art Print, Framed or Unframed
Description · 12 paragraphs

Ferrari Enzo 2003 poster by Lombardia Automobili. Giclée art print, shipped rolled or framed and ready to hang. A gift for anyone who loves the Ferrari Enzo.

- Giclée on 200 gsm fine art paper, archival pigment inks - 4 sizes, from 30 x 40 cm to 75 x 100 cm (12 x 16 in to 30 x 40 in) - Unframed, or in a solid wood frame in 8 colours, ready to hang - Free shipping to the EU, UK, US and Australia - US orders are made and shipped in the US: no import fees - Arrives damaged? Replaced at no cost

A plain black field with the long red script Enzo across its lower half gives the artwork presence across a room. The red Enzo, seen head-on with both butterfly doors raised, invites a closer look. Above a desk or in a garage lounge, it's a conversation piece for anyone who knows what that name means.

The subject is the 2003 Ferrari Enzo in Rosso Corsa, seen head-on with both butterfly doors raised, with its Formula 1-style nose and yellow Ferrari shield, narrow headlamps and black mesh air intakes. Named after the company's founder, it carries a 6.0-litre V12 behind the cabin in a carbon-fibre chassis, and its body was styled at Pininfarina by Ken Okuyama.

Every car was a drawing before it was a car. We start with the finished car and draw it back to that first decision, until the proportion is honest and the stance is right and you would know it from across the room without a badge.

THE PRINT Giclée on 200 gsm enhanced matte fine art paper, archival pigment inks. Portrait, 3:4. Choose the size in the menu: 30 x 40 cm (12 x 16 in), 45 x 60 cm (18 x 24 in), 60 x 80 cm (24 x 32 in) or 75 x 100 cm (30 x 40 in).

FRAMED Solid wood classic frame, satin-laminated, 20 mm face, 22 mm deep, Perspex glazing, no mount. Black, white, natural oak, brown, dark grey, light grey, antique silver or antique gold. Arrives assembled with the hanger fitted, ready to hang, flat in a rigid box with the corners protected. Framed outer size is the print size plus about 1.5 cm on each side.

UNFRAMED Shipped rolled in a rigid tube.

DELIVERY Dispatched within 3 to 6 business days, then tracked shipping to your door. Returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery; the buyer pays the return postage. Anything that arrives damaged is reprinted at no cost.

COLOUR Colours are printed from the original artwork file. Paper and screens differ a little, so allow for a slight difference.

QUESTIONS Which size should I choose? 30 x 40 cm suits a shelf or a small wall, 45 x 60 cm sits above a desk or sideboard, 60 x 80 cm and 75 x 100 cm fill the wall above a sofa or bed. Is a frame included? Only if you pick a frame colour. "No frame (rolled)" is the print on its own. Can it be sent as a gift? Yes, enter the recipient's address at checkout and it ships straight to them.

The year and the details describe the car. This is a contemporary artwork by an independent design house, not a period factory document, and not affiliated with Ferrari.
