Lot title
DB5 - GRAN TURISMO - 60 × 80 cm - Edition of 77
Description · 6 paragraphs

You’re bidding on a 60 × 80 cm GRAN TURISMO poster by Lombardia Automobili, featuring the 1964 Aston Martin DB5. The artwork is 100% unique combination of a detailed illustration of the car and our own layout and typography. A white frame and Perspex glazing are included; poster and frame are shipped together with care.

Warm parchment, a sweeping tobacco brushstroke and classic serif ASTON MARTIN / DB5 lettering give the artwork presence across a room. The dark graphite DB5 on its wet evening road, its chrome wire wheels, grille and wings badge invite a closer look. Above a desk or in a garage lounge, it’s a conversation piece for anyone who knows what those three characters mean.

Lombardia Automobili is an independent automotive design house. This artwork is limited to 77 physical prints in total across all sizes, colour variants, framing options and sales channels. GRAN TURISMO is made for people who appreciate the design of a car as much as the way it drives.

The subject is the 1964 DB5, the definitive 1960s Aston Martin grand tourer. Its 4.0-litre straight-six, Superleggera aluminium body built on the Touring of Milan principle and long, unhurried lines made it a defining Aston Martin. Around a thousand were built between 1963 and 1965, and this is the closed saloon.

Sheet size: 60 × 80 cm. Nominal assembled frame dimensions: 63 × 83 cm. This is a contemporary, retro-inspired studio edition.

An independent artwork by Lombardia Automobili, not affiliated with or endorsed by Aston Martin Lagonda.

Every car was a drawing before it was a car. We start with the finished car and draw it back to that first decision, until the proportion is honest and the stance is right and you would know it from across the room without a badge.

THE PRINT Giclée on 200 gsm enhanced matte fine art paper, archival pigment inks. Portrait, 3:4. Choose the size in the menu: 30 x 40 cm (12 x 16 in), 45 x 60 cm (18 x 24 in), 60 x 80 cm (24 x 32 in) or 75 x 100 cm (30 x 40 in).

FRAMED Solid wood classic frame, satin-laminated, 20 mm face, 22 mm deep, Perspex glazing, no mount. Black, white, natural oak, brown, dark grey, light grey, antique silver or antique gold. Arrives assembled with the hook fitted, ready to hang, flat in a rigid box with the corners protected. Framed outer size is the print size plus about 3 cm on each side.

UNFRAMED Shipped rolled in a rigid tube.

DELIVERY Printed to order and dispatched within 3 to 5 business days, then tracked shipping to your door. We cannot take returns, but anything that arrives damaged is replaced at no cost.
