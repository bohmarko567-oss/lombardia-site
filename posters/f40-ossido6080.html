Ferrari F40, Serie Ossido, Tavola II. Limited edition, framed.

This F40 is drawn as a large three-quarter study with its front, side and rear elevations beside it, projection lines and dimension arrows on an oxide-red ground. The title block carries the factory figures for the 1987 car: 4430 x 1970 x 1124 mm on a 2450 mm wheelbase, 2936 cc twin-turbo V8, 478 CV, 321 km/h.

This one comes framed: a white frame, a slim 20 mm profile with mitred corners, chosen to lift the oxide-red ground. 60 x 80 cm, 600 x 800 mm print, 630 x 830 mm framed.

Every sheet is signed. Printed on a smooth satin stock. Shipped flat with its frame, corners protected, in a rigid box; the print drops into the frame and it is ready to hang.

Serie Ossido is our red series: one ground, one ink, one drawing standard.

Ferrari F40, drawn by Lombardia Automobili.

The title block carries the factory figures for the 1987 car: 4430 x 1970 x 1124 mm on a 2450 mm wheelbase, 2936 cc twin-turbo V8, 478 CV, 321 km/h. One car per sheet, its proportions taken from the manufacturer's own dimensions.

Every car was a drawing before it was a car. We start with the finished car and draw it back to that first decision, until the proportion is honest and the stance is right and you would know it from across the room without a badge.

THE PRINT Giclée on 200 gsm enhanced matte fine art paper, archival pigment inks. Landscape, 4:3. Choose the size in the menu: 40 x 30 cm (16 x 12 in), 60 x 45 cm (24 x 18 in), 80 x 60 cm (32 x 24 in) or 100 x 75 cm (40 x 30 in).

Arrives assembled with the hook fitted, ready to hang, flat in a rigid box with the corners protected. Framed outer size is the print size plus about 3 cm on each side.

UNFRAMED Shipped rolled in a rigid tube.

DELIVERY Packed with care and dispatched within 1 to 3 business days, then tracked shipping to your door. We cannot take returns, but anything that arrives damaged is replaced at no cost.

The year and the factory figures describe the car. This is a contemporary artwork by an independent design house, not a period factory document, and not affiliated with Ferrari S.p.A.
