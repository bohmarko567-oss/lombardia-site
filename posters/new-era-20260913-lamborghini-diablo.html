Lot title
Diablo - FORMA / MOTION - 45 × 60 cm - Edition of 100
Description · 6 paragraphs

You’re bidding on a 45 × 60 cm FORMA / MOTION poster by Lombardia Automobili, featuring the 1991 Lamborghini Diablo. The artwork is 100% unique combination of a detailed illustration of the car and our own layout and typography. A white frame and Perspex glazing are included; poster and frame are shipped together with care.

Near-black violet, a tall electric-yellow aperture and sharp DIABLO typography give the artwork presence across a room. The yellow Diablo on its wet reflective floor and its technical details invite a closer look. Above a desk or in a garage lounge, it’s a conversation piece for anyone who knows what that name means.

Lombardia Automobili is an independent automotive design house. This artwork is limited to 100 physical prints in total across all sizes, colour variants, framing options and sales channels. FORMA / MOTION is made for people who appreciate the design of a car as much as the way it drives.

The subject is the 1991 Diablo, the first-generation rear-wheel-drive car with pop-up headlamps and no rear wing. Its mid-mounted V12, wide wedge body and scissor doors made it a defining Lamborghini. The early Diablo put its emphasis on raw speed and presence rather than driver aids.

Sheet size: 45 × 60 cm. Nominal assembled frame dimensions: 48 × 63 cm. This is a contemporary, retro-inspired studio edition.

An independent artwork by Lombardia Automobili, not affiliated with or endorsed by Lamborghini.
