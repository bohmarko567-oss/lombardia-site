Lot title
850 T-5R Estate - FORMA / MOTION - 80 × 60 cm - Edition of 100
Description

You’re bidding on a 80 × 60 cm FORMA / MOTION poster by Lombardia Automobili, featuring the 1995 Volvo 850 T-5R Estate. The artwork is 100% unique combination of a detailed illustration of the car and our own layout and typography. A white frame and Perspex glazing are included; poster and frame are shipped together with care.

Cream Yellow, warm ivory and oversized 850 typography give the artwork presence across a room. Diagonal cobalt bands bring a sense of speed to the boxy estate, while its five-spoke wheels and crisp details invite a closer look. Above a desk or in a garage lounge, it’s a conversation piece for anyone who appreciates an unexpected performance car.

Lombardia Automobili is an independent automotive design house. This artwork is limited to 100 physical prints in total across all sizes, colour variants, framing options and sales channels. FORMA / MOTION is made for people who appreciate the design of a car as much as the way it drives.

The 1995 Volvo 850 T-5R combined a turbocharged five-cylinder engine with the practical shape of an estate. Cream Yellow paint, graphite-grey five-spoke wheels and its distinctive front spoiler gave the model an unmistakable identity.

Sheet size: 80 × 60 cm. Nominal assembled frame dimensions: 83 × 63 cm. This is a contemporary, retro-inspired studio edition.

An independent artwork by Lombardia Automobili, not affiliated with or endorsed by Volvo.
