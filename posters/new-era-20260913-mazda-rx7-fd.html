RX-7 FD - FORMA / MOTION - 80 × 60 cm - Edition of 100
Description · 6 paragraphs

You’re bidding on a 80 × 60 cm FORMA / MOTION poster by Lombardia Automobili, featuring the 1993 Mazda RX-7 FD. The artwork is 100% unique combination of a detailed illustration of the car and our own layout and typography. A white frame and Perspex glazing are included; poster and frame are shipped together with care.

Pale celadon, deep red brush arcs and tall serif RX-7 typography give the artwork presence across a room. The red FD on its reflective floor and its technical details invite a closer look. Above a desk or in a garage lounge, it’s a conversation piece for anyone who knows what that name means.

Lombardia Automobili is an independent automotive design house. This artwork is limited to 100 physical prints in total across all sizes, colour variants, framing options and sales channels. FORMA / MOTION is made for people who appreciate the design of a car as much as the way it drives.

The subject is the 1993 RX-7, the third-generation FD. Its twin-rotor 13B-REW rotary engine with sequential twin turbochargers, low kerb weight and flowing bodywork made it a defining Mazda. The FD put its emphasis on balance and driver involvement rather than outright size.

Sheet size: 80 × 60 cm. Nominal assembled frame dimensions: 83 × 63 cm. This is a contemporary, retro-inspired studio edition.

An independent artwork by Lombardia Automobili, not affiliated with or endorsed by Mazda.

Every car was a drawing before it was a car. We start with the finished car and draw it back to that first decision, until the proportion is honest and the stance is right and you would know it from across the room without a badge.

THE PRINT Giclée on 200 gsm enhanced matte fine art paper, archival pigment inks. Landscape, 4:3. Choose the size in the menu: 40 x 30 cm (16 x 12 in), 60 x 45 cm (24 x 18 in), 80 x 60 cm (32 x 24 in) or 100 x 75 cm (40 x 30 in).

FRAMED Solid wood classic frame, satin-laminated, 20 mm face, 22 mm deep, Perspex glazing, no mount. Black, white, natural oak, brown, dark grey, light grey, antique silver or antique gold. Arrives assembled with the hook fitted, ready to hang, flat in a rigid box with the corners protected. Framed outer size is the print size plus about 3 cm on each side.

UNFRAMED Shipped rolled in a rigid tube.

DELIVERY Printed to order and dispatched within 3 to 5 business days, then tracked shipping to your door. We cannot take returns, but anything that arrives damaged is replaced at no cost.

The year and the details describe the car. This is a contemporary artwork by an independent design house, not a period factory document, and not affiliated with Mazda.
