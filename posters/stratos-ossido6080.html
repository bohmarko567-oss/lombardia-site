Lancia Stratos HF, Serie Ossido, Tavola XVIII. Limited edition, framed.

This Stratos HF is drawn as a large three-quarter study with its front, side and rear elevations beside it, projection lines and dimension arrows on an oxide-red ground. The title block carries the factory figures for the 1973 car: 3710 x 1750 x 1114 mm on a 2180 mm wheelbase, 2418 cc V6 DINO, 190 CV.

This one comes framed: a white frame, a slim 20 mm profile with mitred corners, chosen to lift the oxide-red ground. 60 x 80 cm, 600 x 800 mm print, 630 x 830 mm framed.

Every sheet is signed. Printed on a smooth satin stock. Shipped flat with its frame, corners protected, in a rigid box; the print drops into the frame and it is ready to hang.

Serie Ossido is our red series: one ground, one ink, one drawing standard.
