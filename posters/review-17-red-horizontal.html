Lot title
NSX NA1 5MT - FORMA / PRECISION - 80 × 60 cm - 1of200
Description

You’re bidding on an 80 × 60 cm FORMA / PRECISION poster by Lombardia Automobili, featuring the 1990 Honda NSX NA1 5MT. The artwork is 100% unique combination of a detailed illustration of the car and our own layout and typography. A white frame and Perspex glazing are included; poster and frame are shipped together with care.

Warm ivory, deep charcoal and oversized NSX typography give the artwork presence across a room. The red Honda and its low, clean silhouette invite a closer look. Above a desk or in a garage lounge, it’s a conversation piece for anyone who knows what those three letters mean.

Lombardia Automobili is an independent automotive design house. FORMA / PRECISION limited edition 1of200 is made for people who appreciate the design of a car as much as the way it drives.

The subject is the 1990 NSX NA1 5MT, the original five-speed manual NSX. Its unmistakable silhouette, mid-mounted 3.0-litre VTEC V6 and focus on driver response made it a defining Honda. The C30A engine was developed specifically for the NSX.

Sheet size: 80 × 60 cm. Nominal assembled frame dimensions: 83 × 63 cm. This is a contemporary, retro-inspired studio edition.

An independent artwork by Lombardia Automobili, not affiliated with or endorsed by Honda Motor Co., Ltd.
